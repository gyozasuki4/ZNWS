Operations instruction manual

Task-based guidance for workstations, situational awareness, Hazard Services, product follow-ups, hydro operations, and administration.

Operational note: Confirm current data, timestamps, geometry, wording, and distribution before issuance. This guide describes ZASNet workflows; governing directives and local policy remain authoritative.

Start-of-shift checklist

  1. Confirm the active WFO and whether the workstation is in operational or Practice Mode.
  2. Open Data & Server and review server/feed health, especially radar and warning distribution.
  3. Center the map on the CWA and load the map setup appropriate for the expected hazards.
  4. Check the latest radar timestamp. A red frame or stale-data notice requires verification before warning decisions.
  5. Review active ZASNet products, NWS alerts, and any existing edit locks before starting new work.

Workspaces and mutual aid

Each workspace tab is a separate operational environment with its own WFO, map view, radar, panels, and draft state. Issued alerts remain shared.

  1. Select + above the map and enter the three-letter WFO ID.
  2. Use that office’s tab when creating or editing its products.
  3. Enable Mutual Aid only when authorized. Products remain attributable to the issuing/operated office.
  4. Closing or switching tabs does not cancel issued products, but save unfinished drafts first.

Map, radar, and layers

  • Map panels are independent unless Link panel navigation is enabled.
  • Use Layers to control visibility, opacity, ordering, and favorites. Saved setups retain panel, radar, and layer choices.
  • Zone-based alerts display as solid fills without outlines. Polygon products use their actual warning geometry.
  • Public and Ops maps may use different controls, but the underlying active product inventory is shared.
  • A red radar timeline frame indicates an unavailable or failed frame. Select another frame and verify the scan time/feed health.

Hazard Services workflow

  1. Validity: choose the product, action, start, and expiration.
  2. Geometry: place/track the storm, draw a polygon, or enable zone selection. Use Select All Zones only when the whole CWA truly applies.
  3. Details: enter only fields belonging to that hazard. Product templates determine where each value appears.
  4. Locations: review automatically selected counties, zones, communities, and pathcast output.
  5. Review: read the entire product. Resolve every placeholder and checklist failure before issuing.

The review editor allows narrative refinement. Protected headers, VTEC, geometry, and coded tags must not be changed manually.

Hydro and river gauges

Areal and flash-flood products

  • Flash Flood Warnings use warning area, Until, basis, HAZARD, SOURCE, IMPACT, LAT…LON, and applicable coded tags.
  • CONSIDERABLE and CATASTROPHIC are exceptional damage-threat levels. CATASTROPHIC requires Flash Flood Emergency wording and strong supporting evidence.
  • Areal Flood Warnings and Flood Advisories use WHAT, WHERE, WHEN, IMPACTS, and ADDITIONAL DETAILS.

Automatic gauge import

  1. Enable River gauges in Layers.
  2. Click a gauge marker and choose Use in Hazard Services.
  3. The system opens an Areal Flood Warning by default, or fills the current Flood Advisory.
  4. Review the NWSLI, observation time/value, forecast crest/time, trend, flood category, thresholds, and available impacts.
  5. Select the confirmation checkbox. Use Refresh gauge immediately before review if time has elapsed.

Gauge values come from NOAA/NWPS. Missing forecast values are shown as unavailable and must not be invented. Forecast-point FLW/FLS products require gauge-specific review even when values are imported automatically.

Updates and follow-ups

CON — Continue
Conditions and expiration remain in effect; update the current basis and impacts.
EXT — Extend
Extend valid time when the hazard will persist. Verify the new end time and wording.
CAN — Cancel
Use when the threat has ended before expiration. State why when appropriate.
EXP — Expire
Use at the scheduled end when the threat no longer warrants continuation.

Do not use a correction to change warned area, event time, or other VTEC-linked information. Use the appropriate lifecycle action or replacement product.

Issuance and distribution

  1. Complete the pre-issue checklist and read the reviewed text from top to bottom.
  2. Verify office, product/action, ETN, expiration, counties/zones, geometry, tags, and CTAs.
  3. Issue once. Wait for the distribution receipt instead of repeatedly pressing Issue.
  4. Confirm required destinations such as the Ops inventory, public map, Mattermost, placefile, and GR warning server when applicable.

Practice Mode products remain Ops-only and are not sent to public or external distribution destinations.

Administration

  • The warning editor controls configurable section wording, optional sections, custom fields, placement tokens, and product defaults.
  • Load an existing template before editing so configured field blocks appear in WHAT/WHERE/WHEN and related sections.
  • Alert display priority controls which overlapping product appears above another on public maps.
  • Access Audit requires the audit permission. It records public-page activity separately from operational product issuance.
  • Test template changes in Hazard Services before relying on them operationally.

Troubleshooting

Gauge unavailable
Retry Refresh Gauge, verify NWPS feed health, and use the NOAA hydrograph link. Do not issue stale values without confirmation.

The frame failed or is unavailable. Select a valid frame and check Data & Server.
New field is absent from text
Confirm its placement token exists in the applicable section template, then regenerate Review.
Public alert is missing
Confirm the product was not issued in Practice Mode and inspect the distribution receipt.
Forbidden / permission required
Confirm the signed-in account’s required group or permission; logging in alone does not grant administrative capabilities.

    Hazard Services guide
    Storm placement, polygons, and hotkeys
    
        
    
          
    Hotkeys
    
          
    • H Open Hazard Services.
    • I Toggle radar inspect/query (hover values on precip/velocity).
    • 1 Place or reposition the storm marker; for static hazards, start or reopen area drawing.
    • 2 Edit the current polygon using its yellow vertex handles.
    • 3 Open Review and Issue—or Review Update for a loaded warning.
    • 4 Redraw the polygon using the current frame-linked storm motion.
    • 5 Trim the polygon to the selected CWA.
    • Enter or double-click finishes the initial polygon and leaves its handles editable.
    • Delete removes the selected or most recent point; right-click a handle to remove it.
    • Escape cancels the current map tool.
    
          
    Single cell
    
          
    1. Place the storm marker on the current cell. A compact editable footprint appears.
    2. Move to an earlier radar frame and drag that frame’s linked dot onto the cell.
    3. Move to a later frame and drag its linked dot onto the same cell.
    4. The fitted track updates the polygon through its expiration, including the centerline and time bars.
    
          
    Line of storms
    
          
    1. Select Line as the storm shape.
    2. Drag the two endpoint dots to the top and bottom of the current storm line.
    3. Use the frame-linked center dots on at least two radar frames to establish motion.
    4. The line and its endpoints are projected through expiration to form the warning polygon.
    
          
    After automatic placement, drag the yellow handles to refine the polygon. Changing duration, direction, or speed recalculates its forecast length while retaining manually selected cell widths.
